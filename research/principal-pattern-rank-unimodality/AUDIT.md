# Scope and review

## Target identity

The universal statement is Vatter’s Conjecture 2.1 about **principal** downsets. The counterexample to McNamara and Steingrímsson’s general-interval conjecture uses a lower endpoint of length 109,051. Vatter’s Conjecture 2.6 further restricts the top to layered permutations. Chen and Zhang–Zhang have results for particular composition families. The present random-host theorem covers asymptotically almost every permutation of each large size and leaves the exceptional hosts open. The monotone-interval observation uses existing results in a different deterministic range. [Original source and update](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.16355v4) · [Prior interval counterexample](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/pull/651) · [Chen](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7385218) · [Yibo Zhang and Zhanhe Zhang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28619)

## Independent mathematical review, 28 November 2026

An independent Codex reviewer read the source and the typical-host proof. They checked the distinction between the exceptional plots, which only need vanishing probability, and the conditional exceptional colorings, for which the proof needs inverse-power bounds. They verified the gap and path estimates, the two-coordinate finite-difference bound and the binomial curvature margin. They also checked joint convergence of the adjacent ratios, their signs for both parities, and the probability of ties. Their search located no earlier public proof of the typical-host result. The older theorem for monotone-interval reconstruction is credited in the deterministic criterion. [Typical hosts](TYPICAL-HOSTS.md) · [Mode](MODE.md) · [Reconstruction](LONG-MONOTONE-INTERVAL.md)

At size eight the reviewer gave the host 12435768, with rank vector (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 9, 5, 1). Its peak at six illustrates why two ranks nearest the middle need not cover the peak even in a unimodal example. Murphy’s example of length 17 also peaks beyond that range. The mode-location observation is distinct from the unimodality conjecture. [Murphy’s example in Vatter](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.16355v4#page=4) · [Exact rank enumerator](check.py)
