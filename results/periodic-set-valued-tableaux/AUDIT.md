# Source, review and verification

## Statement checked

Dal Zovo’s second question in §4 asks about the recurrence and asymptotic enumeration of the generalized tableaux of shifted strips and shifted shapes. Equations (4) and (5) require surjectivity and compare every pair of times. Multiplicities of individual cells are free. The theorem here fixes a positive width and allows every pair of compatible boundary profiles, which includes every translate-periodic zigzag. At each excess it uses the original physical cells as the word alphabet. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29520v1#S4) · [Source PDF, pp. 5–6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.29520#page=5)

The two coordinate conventions have different answers. The literal translation in v1, combined with its product order, gives a factorial lower bound and rules out even an eventual complex constant-coefficient recurrence. In the orientation of the cited ordinary-strip results there is one rational constant-coefficient recurrence per width and fixed excess, valid from period zero. The same finite transition graph gives the full two-variable generating function. Positive-slope diagonals can fail to satisfy a one-variable constant-coefficient recurrence. Width one in the positive theorem uses the source’s product order, giving a diagonal chain. Sun’s ordinary strip statement starts at width two.
