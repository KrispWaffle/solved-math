# Set-valued tableaux on periodic shifted diagrams

A single finite transfer counts every allowable number of repeated entries at every width, including diagrams with different initial and terminal outlines. It gives a constant-coefficient recurrence in the number of periods for each fixed excess. The asymptotic formulas cover fixed excess, growing sublinear excess, and positive finite excess per cell. At superlinear excess the proof gives the exponential growth rate. In the literal coordinates printed in the source the counts grow superexponentially, even for ordinary tableaux. ## Target and entry search, 27 November 2026

Dal Zovo asks in the second open question of §4 whether the recurrence and asymptotic results for ordinary tableaux of shifted strips and shifted shapes extend to the generalized numbers $f^D(n)$. In equations (4)–(5), a word of $n$ cells must use every cell of $D$, and every occurrence of a smaller cell must precede every occurrence of a strictly larger cell. Thus $n$ can exceed the cell count by an arbitrary amount. For a fixed strip width and any two allowed boundary outlines, the intervening diagrams grow by translates of the same period. An answer should count the original words for every such diagram, recover the ordinary counts at zero excess, give an effective recurrence as the diagram grows, and identify precisely the regime of its asymptotic formula. [Dal Zovo, v1, §§2.2, 3.1 and 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29520v1#S4)

The word model belongs to the established theory of standard set-valued tableaux. The ordinary tableau–exclusion-walk correspondence is Dal Zovo’s Theorem 1; recurrences and asymptotics for ordinary periodic tableaux are due to Sun, Chan and others. Set-valued $P$-partitions, barely set-valued formulas, and the fixed-alphabet recurrence of stretched tableaux are also precedents. A formula that enumerates one fixed finite poset, or an ordinary-tableau transfer that never permits repetitions, does not by itself give a recurrence uniform in the growing diagram and its unbounded excess. Such fixed-shape formulas are dependencies. [Lam and Pylyavskyy](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.2189) · [Chan](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05594) · [Alexandersson](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.02717) · [Kim, Schlosser and Yoo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.03253)

Green had already identified the underlying periodic heap and its ordinary exclusion moves. The Hecke-word model of Monical, Pankow and Yong counts arbitrary extensions of fixed ordinary skew shapes. Lam and Pylyavskyy’s linear multi-extensions suppress immediate repetitions, which can be restored by expanding runs. Gorbounov, Korff and Mihalcea use a cylindrical identification of entries. The results here use Green’s ordinary transitions and keep the repeatable cells of the finite interval, and derive a width-bounded transfer and the excess asymptotics.
