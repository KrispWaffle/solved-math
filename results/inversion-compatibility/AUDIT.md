# Scope, priority and independent review

The target is the explicit gap between Theorems 7.8 and 7.9 in §7.1 of Claesson, Linusson, Ulfarsson and Verkama, arXiv:2604.01143v2 (6 November 2026). The definition covers every decomposable or almost decomposable 1324-avoider, at all inversion counts, with the specified choice of boundary. This package gives an intrinsic classification for singleton restrictions and polynomial-time recognition for arbitrary explicitly listed finite bases. The separate all-length monotonicity Problem 8.1 has a prior solution as a consequence of the Schröder recurrence, detailed below. ## The separate all-length question

A second-stage search by two independent readers identified an older answer to the existence question in CLUV Problem 8.1. Inverse followed by complement maps $1324,1342$ to $4231,4132$ and replaces the inversion count $k$ by $\binom n2-k$. Write $S_m(q)$ for the inversion polynomial of these Schröder permutations of length $m+1$. In the published recurrence for $S_n$ the summand of largest shift is $q^nS_{n-1}$ and every other summand has nonnegative coefficients. Thus $S_n(q)-q^nS_{n-1}(q)$ is coefficientwise nonnegative. Reversing the coefficients in degree $\binom{n+1}2$ gives $A_{n+1}(q)-A_n(q)$ for the original pair, for every $n\ge1$. The empty length is immediate. This settles the existential Problem 8.1 as a consequence of prior work. It receives no new-discovery entry. [Bandlow, Egge and Killpatrick, recurrence and attribution to Barcucci, Del Lungo, Pergola and Pinzani](https://www.ericegge.net/papers/schroderperm.pdf#page=3) · [Question](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.01143v2#page=36)

The independently derived coordinate version is also earlier. In a reversed position-indexed Lehmer code, remove entries equal to their positive slot index. The remaining digits are weakly increasing precisely for the original pair. Under the same symmetry these are exactly Callan’s nonfixed entries of the truncated coinversion table. Adding an initial zero among them and keeping the fixed coordinates amounts to wrapping a Schröder path in north and east steps. This is a different growth map from the prioritized Linusson–Verkama injection classified here. [Callan, proposition on p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.05571#page=2)
