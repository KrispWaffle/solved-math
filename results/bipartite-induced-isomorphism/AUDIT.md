## Neighboring hardness results and independent review, 28 November 2026

Bodlaender and coauthors use large stars to select candidate bins in a reduction for ordinary subgraph isomorphism on bounded-diameter forest hosts. Gima and coauthors observe that the proof can be adapted to induced subgraph isomorphism at treedepth three. Their six-branch host contains the subdivided claw as an induced subgraph, which excludes it from the bipartite permutation class. These papers precede the general star-selection idea and the bounded-treedepth hardness. Our result concerns two-center host components and the boundary conjectured for bipartite permutation graphs. [Bodlaender et al., Theorem 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.10670#page=5) · [Gima et al., §3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.09414#page=6) · [Hanaka et al., introduction](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.06383#page=2)

An independent mathematical referee checked the first prefix/suffix reduction against the original statement, including the possibility that an uncolored biclique reverses its shores. That referee then devised the two-center improvement and its star-only version. A separate referee and the author of the first construction audited the new proof. They checked the star centers, forced common leaf, exact saturation, repeated weights and both directions. The separate referee also checked the older six-branch reduction, the published positive case and the exact hypotheses of numerical matching. Two independent small-instance computations agreed on all 4,085 sorted instances tested, including ten negative instances that pass both preliminary checks. The explicitly bounded choice of $H$ records the unary complexity bound.

## Independent formal review

The final statement follows the journal convention of time polynomial in the combined vertex counts. Its two adjacency matrices with unary order headers have polynomially equivalent length. The preprint measures time in the host order and separately says the polynomial is independent of the pattern order. When the orders are available initially, an oversized pattern can be rejected immediately.

The independent referee rebuilt the four Lean modules in an empty project output directory with warnings treated as errors and checked an independent consumer against the expanded host-only quantifiers. The consumer also checked the packing capacities, the two orders and path exclusion. The axiom reports contained only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
