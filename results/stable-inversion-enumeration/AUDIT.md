# Source, priority and independent review

## Exact question

Problem 8.3 in version 2 of *Inversion monotonicity in subclasses of the 1324-avoiders* asks for the limit sequence of the single classical pattern 1243. The coefficient at $k$ is the eventual number of length-$n$ avoiders with exactly $k$ inversions. The same question is Problem 8.2 in version 1. The proof gives a length-independent formula with finitely many summands at each weight, a bijection, and equality with the actual length-$n$ count whenever $n\ge k+2$. [Version 2, §8](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.01143v2#S8) · [Version 1, p. 42](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.01143v1#page=42) · [Proof](PROOF.md)

## Prior-work searches on 28 November 2026

At target selection, the search covered 1243, its reverse complement 2134, the displayed initial sequence, inversion enumeration, indecomposable avoiders, insertion trees and limit sequences. Verkama’s thesis reduces the stable series to an unknown series for indecomposable avoiders. Franklín's classification covers collections of patterns of length at most three. Nakamura’s catalytic-variable algorithm for the fixed-length inversion polynomial of 1243 already yields each term upon evaluation at a sufficiently large length. Proposition 5.1 of the source gives stabilization by confining the nontrivial sum components. The source credits the earlier two-partition formula for 1324 to Claesson, Jelínek and Steingrímsson. ## Independent mathematical review on 28 November 2026

A separate Codex reviewer, `separator_statement_review`, read the primary source, the manuscript and all four Lean modules. The reviewer checked insertion and deletion of the minimum, the legality of every padding step, both directions of the marked-partition bijection and finiteness at fixed weight. The review sharpened the length estimate to $n\ge k+2$, checked that the path $(k,0)$ witnesses strict failure one length earlier for every $k\ge1$, and identified the previous fixed-length algorithm and the source of the two-sided product. The final manuscript incorporates these findings. The bivariate formula and the general product received written mathematical review. [Reviewed manuscript](PROOF.md)

The reviewer also enumerated avoiders by appending their rightmost value, independently of minimum insertion. The resulting finite coefficients agreed with the bivariate formula in all 399 pairs with $0\le n\le20$ and $0\le k\le18$. The repository checker separately compares 1,311 stable entries, the published ten coefficients, and literal avoidance over every permutation through length eight. [Repository checker](verify.py)

## Formal statement and independent kernel replay

The source type is a permutation of `List.range n` whose inversion count is $k$ and which has no 1243 sublist. On the right, `AtWeight k` contains a weakly decreasing list of positive parts and a list of marks, both mark coordinates strictly decreasing in storage order, with each weight below its level. The formal weight charges each run start exactly once. The theorem equates the cardinalities of these two finite types under the sole bound $k+2\le n$.
