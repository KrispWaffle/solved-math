# Distinct classical patterns in a random permutation

Choose a uniform permutation on $n$ positions. Each subset of positions gives a pattern when its values are replaced by their relative ranks. Write $X_n$ for the number of different patterns so obtained. Biers-Ariel, Godbole and Kelley asked for its expectation in 2017. Borrás-Serrano, Byrne, Godbole and Veimau later asked for its normalized expectation and limiting law. Their first version contains the questions, and their second version withdraws an earlier proposed lower bound. [2017 question, §4(b)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.08661#page=10) · [Original questions, §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.13194v1#S4) · [Version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13194)

**The expected fraction of distinct patterns tends to $e^{-2}$, approximately 13.53%.** For subsets of size $m$ with $m/n\to p$ in $(0,1)$, the expected fraction tends to $\exp(-2(1-p)/p)$. The proof counts the other subsets that give a typical subset’s pattern. With probability tending to one, all of them arise by independent replacements of single entries. [Mean and positive-density theorem](PROOF.md)

The random fraction $X_n/2^n$ itself has a nonconstant limiting distribution. For independent Poisson variables $N_{d,e}$ of mean two, its limit is the product of $(1-2^{-d-e})^{N_{d,e}}$ over all positive $d,e$. In particular $\operatorname{Var}(X_n)/4^n\to e^{-4}(e^{2/9}-1)$. [Distribution and variance](LIMIT-LAW.md)

Across all nonempty lengths, the fraction of distinct patterns converges in law to a single random continuous curve, uniformly after linear interpolation. Its values at different interior densities have strictly positive covariance. At a length approaching a fixed positive fraction below one, the total variation distance from every Poisson law tends to one. When a positive sublinear number of positions is deleted, the scaled loss approaches the number of adjacent positions with consecutive values in the same permutation. That number converges in law to a Poisson variable of mean two. [Full profile](UNIFORM-PROFILE.md) · [Covariance and Poisson approximation](LENGTH-PROFILE.md) · [Deletion deficits](EDGE-LENGTHS.md)
