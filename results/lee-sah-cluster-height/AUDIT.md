## Lean scope

The unconditional theorem `LeeSah.lee_sah_conjecture61 : Conjecture61` uses the finite fraction of actual extensions on which at least one marked rank deviates. `LeeSah.extension_rank_tail_all` proves the exponential estimate for every size, with strictly positive constants. [Final proof](../../lean/SolvedMath/ClusterLimit.lean) · [Definitions](../../lean/SolvedMath/ClusterHeight.lean)

The continuous-label construction gives equal mass to each legal order. Integrating the lower and upper branch chains gives the exact spine marginal, including the cases of empty branches. The beta quantile sampler and its product density give the reference law. The formal argument uses the margin $B/(8N)$, where $B$ is the beta normalizing constant, and the elementary $2N e^{-2N\eta^2}$ order-statistic bound. With probability at least one half, every reference tail sample avoids both boundary bands. The factorial for their strict order cancels between the numerator and denominator. Uniform continuity transfers the estimate to the source grid, and concentration of the full set of labels transfers it to extension ranks.
