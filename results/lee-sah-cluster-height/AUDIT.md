# Scope, prior work and verification

## Opening search on 27 November 2026

The target is Conjecture 6.1 of Mitchell Lee and Ashwin Sah, arXiv:1807.04921v1, and its counterpart in *Advances in Applied Mathematics* 103. Definition 1.3 specifies the original poset $P_n^{m,a,b}$. Section 3 identifies the marked elements $X_i$ and Section 4 defines the inverse beta distribution function. The uniform-in-$i$ random height assertion concerns the original poset. Theorem 1.4 estimates the number of its linear extensions. Definition 2.1 adds two boundary chains to form a different poset $Q_n^{m,a,b}$. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.04921) · [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aam.2018.10.001)

I searched the conjecture number, authors’ names, DOI and arXiv identifier, cluster-poset limit shape, random-extension ranks, spine heights, inverse beta distribution, proofs and counterexamples. The OpenAlex entry for the journal paper returned four citing works. Their subjects were pattern avoidance in rooted forests, lifting the cluster method, Wilf equivalences in forests, and consecutive-pattern equivalence. I also searched the reference repository’s research tree, Mathlib and our catalog and topic refs. I found no earlier proof or disproof of this concentration statement in the sources checked. [Forward citations](https://openalex.org/works?filter=cites:W2884595388) · [Related packing-congruence issue](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/28504)

The proof uses the standard density of order statistics, the original continuous label construction, and the Dvoretzky–Kiefer–Wolfowitz concentration inequality. Its extra comparison takes every intermediate spine coordinate as an observation and bounds the change of density by telescoping products over the windows. Finite examples can check the coordinate model; the limit theorem rests on the all-size probability bound. [Massart](https://doi.org/10.1214/aop/1176990746)

Lee and Sah’s own Section 4, especially Lemma 4.1 and equations (8)–(11), bounds an integral for the modified poset against a spacing law. Part II of Blitvić, Kammoun and Steingrímsson also integrates the attached chains in an overlap-one stack. Both are direct antecedents for the density calculation. Pandey, Kumar and Puri predict a related one-point equilibrium density for an ordered particle system. These works have different conclusions from a simultaneous rank bound for the original poset. [Lee–Sah, §4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.04921) · [Blitvić–Kammoun–Steingrímsson, §8.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.12829v1#S8.SS1) · [Pandey–Kumar–Puri, equation (29)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.10524#page=10)

The reviewer checked the product comparison, lower bound for its normalizer, both concentration estimates, uniformity over the marks, and the first and last marks. The reviewer also located the earlier integral estimates above and independently queried both OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar. This is an independent agent review, with no external refereeing claimed.

The reviewer compared direct quotient-poset counts with exact integrals for 168 stacks, checking 2,413 rational moment identities at 504 marked positions. The repository’s reproducible checker constructs the poset by gluing the original window chains and counts extensions by dynamic programming on its ideals. It independently integrates the proposed density one spine coordinate at a time. For each mark it compares enough exact rational moments to identify the entire finite rank distribution. ## Lean scope

`LeeSah.Conjecture61` states convergence in probability using finite counts of actual linear extensions, the beta quantile and a common bad event over all marks. `LeeSah.density_sandwich` and `LeeSah.tilt_bound` prove the two main change-of-density inequalities. `LeeSah.extension_count_pos` proves that the uniform finite counting denominator is positive. `LeeSah.uniform_cube_tail` proves an exponentially small simultaneous tail for all order statistics of an actual product of uniform random variables, using Hoeffding’s inequality at fixed thresholds. `LeeSah.rank_conventions` compares both normalizations, and `LeeSah.conjecture61_of_tail_bound` derives the source statement under the exponential estimate. The unconditional transfer from discrete extension counts through the order-statistics model supplies the remaining formal work. The full argument appears in the reviewed proof.
